Toyota 4Runner 2026 Models

Toyota 4Runner Limited (Starting at ~$70000)

Under the hood, the standard powertrain is a 2.4-liter turbocharged i-FORCE inline-4 engine pushing 278 horsepower and 317 lb-ft of torque. For enhanced power, buyers can opt for the premium i-FORCE MAX Hybrid setup, which incorporates an electric motor to boost output to 326 horsepower and 465 lb-ft of torque. The vehicle utilizes an 8-speed automatic transmission and offers full-time four-wheel drive (4WD) paired with a Torsen limited-slip center locking differential for excellent winter and foul-weather handling.

Inside, the Limited acts as a comfort-focused powerhouse. It features standard heated and ventilated leather-trimmed seats, a power moonroof, and a massive 14-inch Toyota Multimedia touchscreen equipped with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. Audiophiles will appreciate the premium 14-speaker JBL audio system, which includes a unique, removable JBL Flex portable Bluetooth speaker built directly into the dash. The model also features a fully digital 12.3-inch gauge cluster and a digital rearview mirror.

The drivetrain setup gives buyers the flexibility of rear-wheel drive or a highly capable full-time four-wheel-drive (4WD) system. Full-time 4WD configurations benefit from a Torsen limited-slip center differential with an electronic locking feature to handle tricky conditions. Despite shifting toward on-road luxury and ride comfort with its unique Adaptive Variable Suspension (AVS), the Limited retains plenty of utility with a 6,000-pound maximum towing capacity and 8.8 inches of ground clearance.

Toyota 4Runner Platinum (Starting at ~$75000)

Under the hood, the Platinum comes standard with the high-output i-FORCE MAX 2.4L turbocharged 4-cylinder hybrid engine paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission. This setup delivers a commanding 326 horsepower and 465 lb-ft of torque, offering plenty of low-end grunt. Power is sent through a Full-Time 4-Wheel Drive (4WD) system featuring a standard locking center differential.

To set it apart from off-road variants like the TRD Pro, the Platinum features an exclusive Adaptive Variable Suspension (AVS) system. This allows drivers to shift between multiple drive modes—including "Comfort" for smooth highway cruising and "Sport S+" for a more engaging throttle response. When it comes to utility, it boasts a robust towing capacity of 5,800 lbs (2,631 kg) and comes standard with a comprehensive Tow Tech Package.

Inside the 5-passenger cabin, you are met with luxury-tier dark navy semi-aniline leather seats accented by distinct contrast stitching and honeycomb quilting. The front seats are 12-way power-adjustable, heated, and ventilated, while the second-row outboard seats are also heated. Tech upgrades dominate the dashboard, featuring a 14-inch multimedia touchscreen, a 10-inch digital gauge cluster, a Head-Up Display (HUD), and a 14-speaker JBL audio system with a removable Bluetooth speaker.

On the outside, the Platinum makes its presence known with 20-inch gloss-black painted alloy wheels, a dark chrome tri-bar front grille, and color-keyed overfenders. It also features premium accents like gloss-black roof rails and door handles, power-folding heated side mirrors, a power moonroof, and fixed running boards.

Toyota 4Runner Trailhunter (Starting at ~$89000)

The 2026 Toyota 4Runner Trailhunter distinguishes itself from the rest of the lineup by being purpose-built specifically for long-distance overlanding right from the factory . While other trims focus on daily comfort or general off-roading, the Trailhunter integrates heavy-duty armor to withstand punishing backcountry terrain. It comes equipped with co-engineered ARB equipment, including a rugged roof rack, steel skid plates, and robust rock rails that safeguard the vehicle's underbody during technical trail navigations.

A major mechanical differentiator lies in its specialized suspension system, which sets it apart from its close sibling, the TRD Pro. While the high-speed TRD Pro utilizes Fox shocks designed for desert running, the Trailhunter is outfitted with an Old Man Emu (OME) 2.5-inch forged monotube suspension with remote rear reservoirs. This setup is specifically engineered to handle heavy loads, maintaining ride height and stability when the vehicle is weighed down with heavy camping gear and expedition supplies.

Utility and integration are also maximized on the Trailhunter to provide a seamless "turn-key" adventure experience. It features a distinctive high- mounted air intake snorkel to pull clean air into the standard i-FORCE MAX hybrid powertrain, as well as an integrated 20-inch LED light bar and RIGID Industries color-switching LED fog lights for superior nighttime visibility. Additionally, an onboard air compressor is built directly into the cargo are a, allowing adventurers to easily reinflate their tires after airing down for maximum traction on the trail.
