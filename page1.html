Toyota 4Runner 2026 Models

Toyota 4Runner SRC 2026 (Starting at ~$58000)

The SR5 trim keeps the 4Runner's classic durability while avoiding the costly hybrid systems and adaptive dampers found in upper trims, making it the smartest financial choice in the lineup. Its design philosophy prioritizes simplicity, reliability, and low maintenance, which directly benefits long-term ownership.

TNGA-F Platform: The 4Runner now rides on the high-strength steel TNGA-F body-on-frame platform, which it shares with the Tacoma, Land Cruiser, and Tundra. This yields a lighter, significantly more rigid chassis.

Drivetrain Choices: Available in Rear-Wheel Drive (RWD), Part-time 4WD, or Full-time 4WD depending on the trim package.

Maximum Towing: All 2026 models feature a maximum towing capacity of up to 6,000 lbs.

Toyota 4Runner TRD Sport (Starting at ~$60000)

The 2026 Toyota 4Runner TRD Sport bridges the gap between rugged capability and everyday usability, avoiding the complex hybrid setups of upper trims to offer a sharper, street-focused alternative. Its engineering philosophy prioritizes on-road stability and responsive handling, making it a highly practical choice for drivers who spend most of their time on the pavement.

TNGA-F Platform: The 4Runner now rides on the high-strength steel TNGA-F body-on-frame platform, which it shares with the Tacoma, Land Cruiser, and Tundra. This yields a lighter, significantly more rigid chassis that vastly improves ride comfort.

Turbocharged Power: A 2.4L i-FORCE turbocharged four-cylinder engine delivers a punchy 278 horsepower and 317 lb-ft of torque, routing power through a responsive eight-speed automatic transmission.

Drivetrain Choices: Available in Rear-Wheel Drive (RWD) or a robust Part-time 4WD system featuring an electronically controlled two-speed transfer case for tackling bad weather or light trails.

Maximum Towing: Like the rest of the redesigned lineup, the TRD Sport features a maximum towing capacity of up to 6,000 lbs to easily pull trailers, boats, or weekend toys.

Toyota 4Runner TRD Off-Road (Starting at ~$65000)

Under the hood, drivers can choose between the standard i-FORCE 2.4-liter turbocharged 4-cylinder engine delivering 278 horsepower and 317 lb-ft of torque, or upgrade to the available i-FORCE MAX hybrid powertrain which cranks output up to 326 horsepower and 465 lb-ft of torque. Both powerplants route power through a smooth 8-speed automatic transmission paired with a part-time four-wheel-drive system.

For technical trail navigation, the TRD Off-Road model is equipped with a standard locking rear differential that forces both rear wheels to turn at the exact same speed for maximum traction. This is complemented by Multi-Terrain Select to adapt grip settings to surfaces like mud, sand, or rocks, alongside Crawl Control which acts as a low-speed off-road cruise control system.
