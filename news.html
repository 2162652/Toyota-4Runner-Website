Toyota 4Runner News!

What the Toyota 4Runners are introducing...

Toyota has completely reinvented its iconic SUV, ushering in the sixth-generation Toyota 4Runner. Moving away from the aging V6 engine, the newest 4Runners are built on the modern TNGA-F body-on-frame platform shared with the Tacoma and Land Cruiser. Current production focus spans the 2026 model—which is actively arriving at dealerships—and the upcoming 2027 Model Year, which brings minor updates like exclusive new colors.

Powertrains and Performance

The old 5-speed automatic and V6 are officially retired. The upcoming lineup offers two highly efficient, turbocharged four-cylinder options:

• i-FORCE Turbo: A standard 2.4-liter turbocharged engine producing 278 horsepower and 317 lb-ft of torque.

• i-FORCE MAX Hybrid: An available powertrain combining the turbo engine with a 48-hp electric motor. This setup pumps out a massive 326 horsepower and 465 lb-ft of torque.

• Capability: Both engines are mated to an 8-speed automatic transmission, capable of towing up to 6,000 lbs. Fuel economy for the hybrid jumps up to a vastly improved 23-24 MPG combined.

Trim Lineups & Design Lineups

The new 4Runner keeps its signature boxy, muscular stance but tightens the design lines for a more aggressive posture. Crucially, the iconic power roll-down rear window makes its return.

Toyota is offering an expansive trim range (up to 12 variants) to fit different styles:

  • • SR5 & TRD Sport: The entry-level and road-focused models, featuring sleek body-coloured cladding and standard LED lighting.
  • • TRD Pro & Trailhunter: Built for severe off-roading. The Trailhunter arrives overlanding-ready with an intake snorkel and onboard air compressor. For the 2027 model year, Toyota has confirmed an exclusive, rugged "Hot Honey" exterior color specifically for the TRD Pro.
  • • Limited & Platinum: Premium grades boasting upscale amenities like heated/ventilated seating and a heated second row.

    • Release Timing:

    The 2026 models are landing on dealer lots now. The subtly updated 2027 model year versions are expected to begin rolling out between late 2026 and early 2027.

    Pricing & Availability:

    The upcoming Toyota 4Runner lineup spans a wide pricing spectrum to accommodate both casual daily drivers and hardcore off-road enthusiasts. The base-level SR5 gas model is estimated to start around $42,270 to $44,000 USD (roughly $58,660 CAD). For buyers looking at premium, overland-ready, or high-performance hybrid setups, the top-tier TRD Pro and Trailhunter trims are expected to start between $68,400 and $68,750 USD (approximately $72,350 CAD).

    In terms of availability, the first wave of 2026 models is landing on dealership lots right now. If you are looking forward to the subtly updated 2027 model year variants— complete with the newly announced "Hot Honey" colorway—those versions are expected to begin rolling out from the factory between late 2026 and early 2027.
